The Atomic Appliance

Better living through better answers. AI as the sleek, friendly appliance that earns its spot on the counter.
Logo concept
  • Enclosure. A rounded seafoam silhouette like a 1950s radio or stand mixer, wrapped in a polished silver bezel with one curved highlight for the chrome gleam.
  • Dial. A warm white dial face holds a tangerine four-point atomic starburst as the power indicator. The starburst alone becomes the app icon and favicon.
  • Wordmark. "Everyday" in script, with a tangerine "AI" below and a small starburst twinkling beside it.
  • Nameplate. A silver pill under the script reads "& Shannon & Co." in spaced geometric caps. Two small feet make it a real object that sits on a counter.
