Atomic Appliance: Image Prompts

Written for Midjourney v6 and DALL-E 3. Paste the prompt as one paragraph.
A
1950s magazine advertisement
A vintage 1950s American magazine advertisement, full page, in the style of mid-century Saturday Evening Post advertising illustration, painted in gouache and airbrush with visible halftone dots, soft paper grain and slight ink misregistration. A cheerful couple in a bright modern kitchen-office, the woman in a tangerine apron and the man in rolled-up shirtsleeves, lean admiringly toward a glossy seafoam green countertop appliance with rounded corners, polished chrome trim, a round chrome-ringed dial and a small glowing screen. A four-point atomic starburst badge sits on the front of the appliance, and a few more starbursts float in the air. Curved containment lines swoop across the page to frame the scene. Warm cream background with pastel seafoam green, atomic tangerine and polished silver highlights. A large hand-lettered brush script headline at the top reads "Better Living Through Better Answers". Beneath it, a clean geometric sans-serif subhead reads "Meet Everyday AI, the dependable helper for your home and office." Small footer logo reads "Everyday AI & Shannon & Co." Optimistic World's Fair mood, print advertisement layout with generous headline space, crisp vintage print quality, high detail.
Settings. Midjourney: aspect ratio 4:5, Raw style, stylize low (around 100). DALL-E 3: tall portrait size.
Tip. Image models often misspell. Keep the headline area, then set the real type in Yellowtail and Jost in your design tool.
B
Product mockup on a mid-century desk
High-end product photograph of a 1950s-style AI appliance sitting on a walnut mid-century modern desk. The appliance has a glossy pastel seafoam green lacquer enclosure with rounded corners, a polished chrome grille, chrome trim and a four-point atomic starburst badge in atomic tangerine. A large chrome-ringed dial on the front is engraved "Think", with a small round amber glowing display above it and three chunky tangerine push buttons below. A script logo reading "Everyday AI" is inlaid on the chrome nameplate. Around it on the desk: a brass tulip desk lamp, a cream rotary telephone, a tangerine ceramic mug and a stack of linen notebooks. An out-of-focus molded plywood lounge chair and a teak sideboard sit in the background, with sunlight streaming through a tall window. Three-quarter hero angle, soft warm window light from the left, glossy specular reflections on the chrome, shot on an 85mm lens at f/2.8, shallow depth of field, warm Kodachrome color grade, commercial catalog photography, ultra detailed, photorealistic.
Settings. Midjourney: aspect ratio 4:5 or 3:2, stylize medium (around 250). DALL-E 3: square or wide size.
Tip. Run the "Think" dial and nameplate as separate close-up shots, then composite the real logo on top.
