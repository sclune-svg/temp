Paste this file (or attach it) into a new Cowork session to continue the project.

## Project in one paragraph
Visual identity for "Everyday AI & Shannon & Co." The concept demystifies modern AI through an optimistic, tactile 1950s lens: AI as a reliable, user-friendly, physical utility rather than abstract magic. Two directions are being explored. The work so far is a Claude Design canvas with four artboards (a brand kit and an image-prompt board for each direction).

## Direction 1: The Atomic Appliance
Vibe: 1950s domestic optimism, "better living through technology," the World's Fair. AI as a sleek, friendly home or office appliance.
Visual elements: chrome finishes, four-point atomic starbursts, curved containment lines, glossy pastel enclosures, enthusiastic 1950s illustration.

**Fonts (Google Fonts):** Yellowtail (header script), Jost (body geometric sans, weights 400, 500, 600).

**Colors:**
| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Seafoam | #78C9B0 |
| Secondary | Atomic Tangerine | #F4772E |
| Accent | Polished Silver | #C5CDD3 |
| Supporting | Deep teal ink (all text) | #17413F |
| Supporting | Warm white (paper) | #F6F1E7 |

Set text in ink, never in seafoam or tangerine.

**Logo concept:**
- Rounded seafoam appliance enclosure (like a 1950s radio or stand mixer) with a polished silver chrome bezel and one curved highlight.
- Round warm-white dial on the left holding a tangerine four-point starburst as the power indicator. The starburst alone is the app icon and favicon.
- "Everyday" in Yellowtail script, with a tangerine "AI" below and a small starburst twinkle beside it.
- Silver nameplate pill reading "& SHANNON & CO." in spaced Jost caps. Two small feet make it a physical object.

**Prompt A, 1950s magazine ad:**
A vintage 1950s American magazine advertisement, full page, in the style of mid-century Saturday Evening Post advertising illustration, painted in gouache and airbrush with visible halftone dots, soft paper grain and slight ink misregistration. A cheerful couple in a bright modern kitchen-office, the woman in a tangerine apron and the man in rolled-up shirtsleeves, lean admiringly toward a glossy seafoam green countertop appliance with rounded corners, polished chrome trim, a round chrome-ringed dial and a small glowing screen. A four-point atomic starburst badge sits on the front of the appliance, and a few more starbursts float in the air. Curved containment lines swoop across the page to frame the scene. Warm cream background with pastel seafoam green, atomic tangerine and polished silver highlights. A large hand-lettered brush script headline at the top reads "Better Living Through Better Answers". Beneath it, a clean geometric sans-serif subhead reads "Meet Everyday AI, the dependable helper for your home and office." Small footer logo reads "Everyday AI & Shannon & Co." Optimistic World's Fair mood, print advertisement layout with generous headline space, crisp vintage print quality, high detail.
Settings: Midjourney v6 aspect ratio 4:5, Raw style, stylize about 100. DALL-E 3 tall portrait.

**Prompt B, product mockup:**
High-end product photograph of a 1950s-style AI appliance sitting on a walnut mid-century modern desk. The appliance has a glossy pastel seafoam green lacquer enclosure with rounded corners, a polished chrome grille, chrome trim and a four-point atomic starburst badge in atomic tangerine. A large chrome-ringed dial on the front is engraved "Think", with a small round amber glowing display above it and three chunky tangerine push buttons below. A script logo reading "Everyday AI" is inlaid on the chrome nameplate. Around it on the desk: a brass tulip desk lamp, a cream rotary telephone, a tangerine ceramic mug and a stack of linen notebooks. An out-of-focus molded plywood lounge chair and a teak sideboard sit in the background, with sunlight streaming through a tall window. Three-quarter hero angle, soft warm window light from the left, glossy specular reflections on the chrome, shot on an 85mm lens at f/2.8, shallow depth of field, warm Kodachrome color grade, commercial catalog photography, ultra detailed, photorealistic.
Settings: Midjourney v6 aspect ratio 4:5 or 3:2, stylize about 250.

## Direction 2: The Space-Age Operator
Vibe: early room-sized mainframes meet the friendly telephone switchboard operator. The brand is the warm human interface to complex, futuristic tech.
Visual elements: toggle switches, analog dials at "Maximum Efficiency," glowing warm cathode lights, Bakelite control panels, punch-tape data streams.

**Fonts (Google Fonts):** Antonio (header, tall condensed sans, 400 and 700), IBM Plex Mono (body, 400, 500, 600).

**Colors:**
| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Bakelite Black | #1B1512 |
| Secondary | Warm Cream | #F1E6CC |
| Accent | Rocket Red | #D6342A |
| Supporting | Cathode Amber (glow, small highlights) | #FFB84D |
| Supporting | Panel Brown (surfaces) | #2A211C |

Rocket red on black is low contrast, so use it for large type and graphics only. Small text stays cream or amber on black.

**Logo concept:**
- Front of a Bakelite console: black panel, cream bevel, four corner screws.
- Cream analog dial with tick marks and a rocket red needle pinned at MAX. The dial alone is the icon, in a black square.
- "EVERYDAY" in cream Antonio with a red "AI", and "& SHANNON & CO." in amber monospace below.
- Three toggle switches and a warm amber cathode lamp.
- A strip of punched paper tape runs under the panel. Holes can spell HELLO in binary as a hidden detail.

**Prompt A, 1950s magazine ad:**
A vintage 1950s industrial magazine advertisement, full page, printed in a limited three-color process of Bakelite black, rocket red and warm cream paper, with coarse halftone texture, worn paper edges and slight ink misregistration. A friendly smiling switchboard operator wearing a telephone headset and a crisp collared blouse sits at a huge Bakelite control console covered in rows of chunky toggle switches, patch cables and glowing warm amber cathode tubes. An oversized analog dial beside her has its needle pinned at "MAXIMUM EFFICIENCY". A ribbon of punched paper tape flows from the console and curls along the bottom of the page. Dramatic limited-palette lighting with a soft amber glow on her face. Headline across the top in tall, condensed, all-caps sans-serif lettering like a vintage machinery warning label: "PLEASE HOLD WHILE WE CONNECT YOU TO THE FUTURE". A small monospaced typewriter caption near the bottom reads "EVERYDAY AI & SHANNON & CO." Retro-futuristic mainframe-era optimism, print advertisement layout, highly detailed vintage illustration, crisp print quality.
Settings: Midjourney v6 aspect ratio 4:5, Raw style, stylize about 100. DALL-E 3 tall portrait.

**Prompt B, product mockup:**
High-end product photograph of a 1950s-style AI terminal sitting on a walnut mid-century modern desk with tapered metal legs. The terminal has a glossy Bakelite black housing with a warm cream faceplate, three chunky chrome-tipped toggle switches, a large analog dial with a polished bezel and a rocket red needle resting at "MAX", and a row of small glowing amber cathode indicator lamps. A strip of punched paper tape feeds out of a slot and curls across the desk. A cream label-maker plate reads "EVERYDAY AI" in tall condensed capitals. Around it: a vintage green glass banker's lamp, a black rotary telephone, a leather-bound ledger and a brass desk bell. A dark paneled office wall blurs behind it. Moody warm tungsten light, a soft pool of amber glow on the desk, deep shadows and glossy reflections on the Bakelite. Three-quarter hero angle, shot on a 100mm macro lens at f/4, shallow depth of field, cinematic color grade, commercial product photography, ultra detailed, photorealistic.
Settings: Midjourney v6 aspect ratio 3:2 or 4:5, stylize about 250. DALL-E 3 wide or square.
